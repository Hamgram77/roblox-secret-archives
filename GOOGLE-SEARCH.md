# Google Search appearance checklist

The site exposes a stable, crawlable 96×96 PNG favicon at:

`https://hamgram77.github.io/roblox-secret-archives/favicon-96.png`

It is declared in the home page and every record page, alongside the site name in WebSite structured data. The icon is square and larger than Google's recommended 48×48 size.

After publishing:

1. Open Google Search Console for the exact property `https://hamgram77.github.io/roblox-secret-archives/`.
2. Inspect the home URL and request indexing.
3. Submit `https://hamgram77.github.io/roblox-secret-archives/sitemap.xml` in the Sitemaps screen.
4. Confirm neither the home page nor `favicon-96.png` is blocked by `robots.txt`.
5. Keep the favicon URL and image stable. Google may take days or weeks to recrawl and does not guarantee that a favicon will appear.

Do not add a site-verification meta tag unless Google Search Console provides the exact owner-specific token.
