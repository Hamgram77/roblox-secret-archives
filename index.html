02 / About

An independent record.

Preserving creator perspectives on the systems that shape development, discovery, and participation.

Roblox Developer Archives is an independent collection created by Hamgram77. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation.

The documents in this collection represent the author’s research and assertions. They are preserved as submitted so readers can inspect the source material directly.

Our mission: preserve creator perspectives, document platform concerns clearly, and turn that public record into constructive pressure for Roblox to make meaningful, lasting improvements to its platform.

03 / Methodology
